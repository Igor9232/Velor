Why Windows says “Unknown publisher”Zašto Windows kaže „Nepoznati izdavač”

v0.2.2 is an indie public beta without a paid Authenticode certificate yet, so Windows SmartScreen shows “Unknown publisher” until Authenticode is added ahead of v1.0. To start it, choose More info then Run anyway. v0.2.2 je nezavisna javna beta koja još nema plaćeni Authenticode certifikat, pa Windows SmartScreen prikazuje „Nepoznati izdavač” dok se Authenticode ne doda prije v1.0. Za pokretanje odaberi Više informacija pa Ipak pokreni.

What you can check instead: every release carries SHA-256 checksums, and update manifests are signed with ECDSA P-256. Velor refuses a manifest whose signature doesn't verify. Što možeš provjeriti umjesto toga: svako izdanje ima SHA-256 sažetke, a manifesti ažuriranja potpisani su ECDSA P-256 ključem. Velor odbija manifest čiji se potpis ne može provjeriti.

Q1Why Windows x64 only?Zašto samo Windows x64?

Velor is built and measured on Windows first: DirectX 12 or Vulkan, Windows shell integration, local Windows OCR and the Windows certificate store for signing. Staying on one platform is how every number on this page stays measured rather than estimated. It runs on Windows 10 1809 or newer, x64. Velor se razvija i mjeri prvo na Windowsima: DirectX 12 ili Vulkan, integracija s Windows ljuskom, lokalni Windows OCR i Windowsovo spremište certifikata za potpisivanje. Upravo zato su sve brojke na ovoj stranici izmjerene, a ne procijenjene. Radi na Windowsima 10 1809 ili novijim, x64.

Q2Does anything leave my PC?Odlazi li išta s mog računala?

Your documents never do. There is no telemetry, no account and no cloud, and the PDF reader contains no HTTP client crates at all: they are banned at build time in deny.toml. Tvoji dokumenti nikad. Nema telemetrije, računa ni oblaka, a PDF čitač nema nijedan HTTP klijentski crate: zabranjeni su već pri izgradnji u deny.toml.

The one request Velor can make is the update check, which you choose in the first-run Setup or in Settings: an HTTPS GET of a signed manifest through Windows' own WinHTTP, with no identifiers and no version number in the request. Jedini zahtjev koji Velor može poslati je provjera ažuriranja, koju biraš pri prvom pokretanju ili u Postavkama: HTTPS GET potpisanog manifesta preko Windowsovog WinHTTP-a, bez identifikatora i bez broja verzije u zahtjevu.

Q3What happens after 1 year?Što se događa nakon godinu dana?

Nothing stops working. Every version released inside your update window keeps working with your key permanently, offline, on any date. If you later want a version released after your window, you can extend updates at a discount, or stay on your version with no obligation. Ništa ne prestaje raditi. Svaka verzija izdana unutar tvog perioda nadogradnji trajno radi s tvojim ključem, offline, na bilo koji datum. Ako kasnije poželiš verziju izdanu nakon tog perioda, nadogradnje možeš produžiti uz popust ili ostati na svojoj verziji bez ikakve obveze.
